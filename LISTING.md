## Description

Maps Agent is the AI marketing team for Google Maps. Its remote MCP server gives Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex,
Gemini and other agents the knowledge base behind it, so an owner or marketer can work on a local business with
sourced answers instead of guesses:

- **Search** guides, answers, research and the glossary on Google Business Profiles, reviews, categories, photos,
  posts and Google Maps rankings.
- **Read** any page in full as markdown, with its canonical URL and the date it was updated.
- **Define** local SEO terms in plain words.
- **Studies** with their method, sample and findings.

Answers are in English and link back to maps-agent.com. Remote Streamable HTTP, read-only, no sign-in, no API key.
